# Accessibility Statement

*Effective from: 20 May 2026 · Version: 1.0 (preliminary)*

> **Note:** The final wording of this statement will be supplied by the Provider after completion of a formal accessibility audit. The content below is based on WCAG 2.1 AA, Directive (EU) 2019/882 (European Accessibility Act, "EAA"), Czech Act No. 424/2023 Coll. and the Provider's internal policy. In case of discrepancy with the [Czech original](/accessibility), the Czech version prevails.

## 1. Accessibility commitment

1.1 **EDU-AI o.p.s.** ("Provider") undertakes to ensure that the EDU AI assistant application ("Application"), its mobile versions (iOS, Android), web version and public pages are accessible to the widest possible range of users including persons with disabilities and functional limitations.

1.2 This statement applies to:

- Web version of the Application at [app.edu-ai.eu](https://app.edu-ai.eu)
- EDU AI assistant mobile app for iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play)
- Public legal pages (terms, privacy, cookies, about, accessibility, AI)

## 2. Compliance status

2.1 The Application is **partially compliant** with WCAG 2.1 level AA. Some features, particularly certain complex interactive educational elements, may still show partial deficiencies, which we are continuously addressing.

2.2 The compliance assessment was based on:

- **WCAG 2.1 level AA** (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines),
- **EN 301 549 V3.2.1** (Accessibility requirements for ICT products and services),
- **Directive (EU) 2019/882** (European Accessibility Act, EAA), applicable from 28 June 2025,
- **Act No. 424/2023 Coll.** on accessibility of products and services (EAA transposition into Czech law).

## 3. Implemented accessibility features

3.1 The Application is designed and developed to allow, within its nature, in particular:

- **Keyboard navigation** — without a pointing device; visible focus indicator.
- **Assistive technologies** — VoiceOver (iOS), TalkBack (Android), NVDA / JAWS / VoiceOver (web).
- **Voice control** — OS voice assistants.
- **Colour contrast** — minimum 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text per WCAG 1.4.3.
- **Text resize** — respects OS font-size settings; readable up to 200% zoom.
- **Text alternatives** — alt text for images, labels for icons, transcripts for A/V (progressively added).
- **Semantic structure** — headings, lists, landmarks and ARIA per WAI-ARIA.
- **Adaptive pace** — pace and time limits adjustable in settings.
- **Difficulty adaptation** — AI and FSRS adapt to user abilities.
- **Reduced motion** — honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Dark mode** — honours system colour-scheme preference.

## 4. AI features and accessibility

4.1 AI outputs are formulated in understandable language and presented in formats compatible with assistive technologies.

4.2 AI features must not disadvantage users with disabilities or specific educational needs. The AI assistant is trained to provide alternative explanations and choice of response format.

## 5. Known issues and exemptions

5.1 Currently known areas under improvement:

- Some older images in educational content lack complete text alternatives.
- Some complex interactive educational elements (drag-and-drop quizzes) have limited keyboard-only support — an alternative form is provided.
- Mathematical expressions generated by AI may not always be fully understandable to screen readers without MathML support.
- Some videos still lack closed captions (CC); captions being added progressively.

5.2 Disproportionate burden (Art. 7 EAA): we currently do not invoke the "disproportionate burden" exemption for any part of the Application.
