## 1. Introductory provisions

1.1 This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how we process personal data of users of the EDU AI assistant application ("Application") and related websites and services.

1.2 We process personal data to the extent necessary, transparently and securely, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), Act No. 110/2019 Coll. on Personal Data Processing, and related legislation.

1.3 This Policy, together with the [Terms of Use](/terms/en), forms the contractual framework for the provision of our services.

## 3. When we are controller and when we are processor

3.1 **Website, marketing and support** — the Provider is the controller of personal data of website visitors, service prospects and persons who contact us.

3.2 **Application — anonymous access** — no personal data identifying you is processed beyond the strictly necessary data described in section 8.

3.3 **Application — school mode** — the educational institution is the controller and the Provider processes their personal data as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR.

3.4 **Application — full access** — the Provider is the controller of personal data of registered users under Art. 24 GDPR.

## 4. What personal data we process

### 4.1 Data provided upon registration and account use

- e-mail address,
- first name and surname or nickname,
- identifier from user code or third-party identity provider (Apple, Google, Microsoft),
- age group,
- information about school, class, year or study level if provided.

### 4.2 Educational and performance data

- answers in lessons and exercises,
- test results, scoring and progress,
- study history and learning preferences,
- notes saved in the Application,
- gamification data (points, badges, levels, learning streaks).

### 4.3 Technical and operational data

- IP address (anonymised or shortened where possible),
- device identifier for analytics not used for advertising,
- device type, OS, App version, language, timestamps,
- crash logs and diagnostic data,
- login, security and feature-use logs.

### 4.4 Analytics and usage data

- which screens, features or content the user uses,
- in-app events, usage statistics, retention and engagement indicators,
- data from strictly necessary cookies and similar technologies (see section 8).

### 4.5 Communication data

- support inquiry content,
- e-mail and other electronic communication,
- feedback, bug reports, content/incident reports.

### 4.6 Data related to AI assistance

- prompts, queries, inputs, submitted texts or attachments,
- AI outputs and technical metadata,
- AI feature interaction data.

> **Safeguards for AI features:** Inputs and outputs are stored for the time necessary for service provision and security. For scientific research (see 6.9) or AI model training, the data is fully anonymised.

### 4.7 Consent and preference data

- consents granted or withdrawn,
- notification and marketing preferences.

### 4.8 Data we do NOT process

- special-category data under Art. 9 GDPR,
- biometric data for unique identification,
- data for behavioural advertising or cross-service profiling,
- data for automated decisions with legal effect on the user.

## 5. Children and minors

5.1 The Application in full-access mode is intended for users 15 years of age or older (§ 7 of Act No. 110/2019 Coll.).

5.2 Users under 15 may use the Application only in school mode.

5.3 If we discover that we have obtained personal data of a user under 15 without an appropriate legal basis, we will delete the data without undue delay.

5.4 Special care for children: minimise processing, no profiling, no behavioural targeting, age-appropriate AI, understandable consent texts.

## 6. Purposes and legal bases

### 6.1 Provision of Application services (full access)

- **Purpose:** registration, account, learning features, study progress evaluation, support.
- **Legal basis:** performance of contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- **Retention:** account duration + 12 months; then erasure.

### 6.2 AI features

- **Purpose:** generating AI outputs based on user inputs.
- **Legal basis:** performance of contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; can be turned off in settings.
- **Retention:** max 90 days unless saved by user.

### 6.3 Human oversight of AI

- **Purpose:** Art. 14 AI Act compliance, handling review requests.
- **Legal basis:** legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.
- **Retention:** 3 years for request records.

### 6.4 School mode processing

- **Purpose:** learning features for pupils per educational institution's instructions.
- **Provider's role:** processor under Art. 28 GDPR.
- **Retention:** for the duration of the school contract.

### 6.5 Inquiries and support

- **Purpose:** answers, technical and user support.
- **Legal basis:** performance of contract and legitimate interest.
- **Retention:** 3 years from last contact.

### 6.6 Analytics and improvement

- **Purpose:** understanding usage, identifying errors, improving UX and content.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, with IP anonymisation and no profiling.
- **Retention:** 26 months aggregated.
- **Objection:** anytime at [gdpr@edu-ai.eu](mailto:gdpr@edu-ai.eu).

### 6.7 Marketing communications

- **Purpose:** information on new features, learning materials, events.
- **Legal basis:** consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, or legitimate interest under § 7(3) of Act No. 480/2004 Coll.
- **Retention:** until withdrawal, max 2 years from last contact.
- **Objection:** anytime at [gdpr@edu-ai.eu](mailto:gdpr@edu-ai.eu).

### 6.8 Accounting

- **Purpose:** invoices, donation records.
- **Legal basis:** legal obligation under Act No. 563/1991 Coll.
- **Retention:** 10 years.

### 6.9 Academic research

- **Purpose:** cooperation with academic partners:
  - Faculty of Education, Charles University,
  - Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, Charles University,
  - University of West Bohemia in Pilsen.
- **Legal basis:** consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, or legitimate interest under Art. 89 GDPR.
- **Safeguards:** pseudonymisation/anonymisation before transfer. No identification of users, no commercial use.
- **Retention:** project duration, max 5 years; anonymised data without time limit.

### 6.10 Legal claims and security

- **Purpose:** misuse protection, incident investigation, claim defence.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
- **Retention:** limitation period (typically 3 years, special cases up to 15 years).

## 10. Automated decision-making and profiling

10.1 We do not carry out automated decision-making with legal effect under Art. 22 GDPR.

10.2 AI features generate educational recommendations and adaptive difficulty that do not constitute binding evaluation. Pedagogical decisions are always made by a teacher or institution.

10.3 Human review of any AI evaluation significantly affecting the user can be requested at [gdpr@edu-ai.eu](mailto:gdpr@edu-ai.eu). Handled within 30 days.
